# Welcome to the Data Science Wiki

This wiki is a shared place for explanations, practical recipes, technical
standards, and lessons learned.

## How articles are organized

Every article has:

- a broad Data Science topic;
- an intended audience level;
- optional tags for tools and methods;
- an author and last-updated date.

Use search when you know what you need, or browse the topic and audience pages
when you want to explore.

## Technical content is welcome

Articles can include highlighted code:

```python
def mean(values: list[float]) -> float:
    return sum(values) / len(values)
```

They can also include tables and equations such as
\(\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i\).
